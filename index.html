How I approach SEO

The same cycle, whether it's one site or a whole portfolio.

01

Audit

Site health checks, crawl errors, and competitive benchmarking to find what's actually holding rankings back.

02

Research

Keyword research focused on high-value, low-competition terms that match real search intent.

03

Optimize

Titles, meta descriptions, headers, and site structure rebuilt around the research — plus speed and mobile checks.

04

Create

Content calendars — blog posts, guides, FAQs — planned around the keywords and the questions people are actually asking.

05

Build authority

Outreach, guest posting, and relationship-building to earn links from relevant, credible sites.

06

Report & adjust

Monthly reporting and quarterly reviews that turn performance data into the next round of strategy.
